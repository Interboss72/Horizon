# Technology news profile

Use this profile for timely developments in software engineering, artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer systems, hardware, open source, and the technology industry.

Typical items include substantial releases, research breakthroughs, security incidents, industry announcements, technical analyses tied to a recent event, and community discussions that add practical insight.

Do not use this profile for content whose primary value is a complete long-form argument, a step-by-step tutorial, a full academic paper, finance-first market coverage, routine updates, or promotional material without substantive information.

## AI LIFEHACKS: Additional Matching Rules

Prioritize practical, verified AI techniques that provide
non-obvious benefits to users.

Focus on:
- Underrated features of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini,
  Nano Banana, and other AI tools.
- Advanced prompting and precise output control.
- Multi-reference image editing and identity preservation.
- AI agents, automation, and cross-platform workflows.
- Local and offline AI tools, especially for Android.
- Image, video, audio, and document processing.
- Techniques that save time, money, or computing resources.
- Privacy-conscious AI workflows.

Reject:
- Generic beginner tips without a new practical benefit.
- Unverified tricks and alleged hidden features.
- Marketing claims without reproducible instructions.
- Old techniques presented as recent discoveries.
- Duplicate advice with no meaningful improvement.

A strong match must offer a concrete, reproducible
workflow supported by reliable documentation or evidence.

Prioritize practical novelty over popularity.
